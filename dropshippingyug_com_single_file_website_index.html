How Dropshipping Works — Step-by-step

Research high-margin, lightweight, low-return items.

Use competitive price + clear photos. Add shipping time in description.

3. Sell & Collect

Customer pays you; retain margin.

Use early profits for ads or a branded store.

Business Models You Can Use

  • Classic Supplier Dropshipping — Source from AliExpress/CJ, list on marketplaces.
  • Print-on-Demand — Use Printful/Printify for custom apparel; only pay per order.
  • Wholesale-to-Consumer — Partner with local wholesalers who accept made-to-order.

Trusted Suppliers & Platforms (Zero Upfront)

These platforms allow you to start listing and selling without buying inventory up-front.

  • AliExpress — Massive catalog, good for testing; long shipping times.
  • CJ Dropshipping — Faster fulfillment centers, integration options.
  • Meesho / GlowRoad (India) — Zero upfront investment, ideal for Indian market.
  • Printful / Printify — Print-on-demand for custom t-shirts, mugs — pay per order.
  • Spocket — US/EU suppliers (free plan available) for faster shipping.
  • Local Marketplaces — Facebook Marketplace, eBay, Etsy, Amazon Individual Seller.

Supplier Checklist

  • High-quality product images & multiple views
  • Clear shipping times & tracking
  • Low return rates and responsive seller communication
  • Option to remove invoices from packages (for private labeling)

Free Marketing Playbook (Organic)

When you can't spend on ads, organic content and direct selling work best.

Social Platforms

  • TikTok — Short demos, before/after, unboxing; include link in bio.
  • Instagram Reels — Product-focused short videos + shop link.
  • Facebook Marketplace — Local sales & quick wins.
  • Pinterest — Product images drive long-term traffic.

Direct Selling

  • WhatsApp catalog + broadcast to warm contacts.
  • Whatsapp/Telegram groups and community selling.
  • Leverage micro-influencers with product-for-post deals.

Conversion Tips

  • Use clear photos and 1–2 short videos per listing.
  • State shipping times honestly — reduces cancellations.
  • Offer fast responses and post-purchase updates.

Operations, Payments & Legal

Payments

  • Use safe payment channels: PayPal, Stripe, Razorpay, UPI (India).
  • Keep clear refund & shipping policies to avoid disputes.

Shipping & Returns

Provide tracking and a clear returns window (e.g., 7–15 days depending on product). When working with AliExpress, expect 15–45 day shipping for some products.

Legal & Taxes

  • Register as required in your country (sole proprietor is common).
  • Collect GST/VAT if threshold applies (India/EU).
  • Keep invoices for each sale and supplier receipts.

Customer Support

  • Provide order status within 24 hours.
  • Use templates for refunds, shipping updates, and replacements.

Scaling from Side Hustle to Business

  1. Track best-sellers carefully and expand listings around winners.
  2. Negotiate with suppliers for better pricing after volume.
  3. Invest early profits in targeted ads to scale the winners.
  4. Consider private-labeling or warehousing for fastest products.

Common Mistakes & How to Avoid Them

  • Not disclosing shipping times — be honest about delivery windows.
  • Poor product images — use high-quality photos or mockups.
  • Ignoring customer messages — fast replies reduce chargebacks.
  • Listing too many unrelated products — focus on a niche initially.

FAQ

Q: Can I start without any money?

A: Yes — using marketplaces and print-on-demand platforms you can list products and only pay suppliers after you sell.

Q: How long before I see profit?

A: It varies. Some sellers make small profits within 1–2 weeks via marketplaces; consistent growth often takes 1–3 months of testing.

Q: What about returns and refunds?

A: Build clear policies. For print-on-demand, replacements are usually handled by the POD provider. For other suppliers, confirm return rules before listing.

Q: Is dropshipping legal?

A: Yes — but follow local tax laws, avoid selling copyrighted or trademarked items, and disclose shipping times authentically.
